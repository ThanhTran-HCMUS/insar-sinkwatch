# 2-Minute Video Pitch Script: InSAR-SinkWatch
**NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2026**  
**Maximum Duration:** 2 minutes (120 seconds)  
**Format:** Screen Recording + Voiceover + On-Camera Intro/Outro  

---

### TIMELINE & STORYBOARD

#### [0:00 - 0:20] The Dramatic Hook: Sinking Cities
* **Visual:** Fast clips of severe street flooding in District 7, Ho Chi Minh City on a sunny day (tidal backwater), followed by zoomed-in satellite imagery covered in impenetrable tropical monsoon clouds.
* **Audio / Voiceover:**
  > *"Every single day, the ground beneath Ho Chi Minh City and the Mekong Delta is sinking by up to 45 millimeters a year—sinking ten times faster than the sea is rising. But under heavy tropical monsoon clouds, optical satellites cannot see it. Until disaster strikes, this silent crisis remains completely invisible."*

#### [0:20 - 0:45] The Space Innovation: NASA-ISRO NISAR
* **Visual:** 3D animation / graphic of the NISAR satellite orbiting Earth, emitting 24-centimeter L-band radar waves penetrating clouds and tropical vegetation down to building foundations.
* **Audio / Voiceover:**
  > *"Meet InSAR-SinkWatch, an AI-powered geohazard observatory powered by the flagship NASA-ISRO NISAR satellite mission. Using repeat-pass L-band radar with a 24-centimeter wavelength, NISAR pierces through dense storm clouds and tree canopies. By analyzing the subtle phase shift between radar passes, we measure vertical surface deformation with millimeter-level precision from space."*

#### [0:45 - 1:20] Live Product Walkthrough
* **Visual:** Smooth screen capture of the `InSAR-SinkWatch` web console running live:
  1. Switching between wrapped InSAR phase fringes (rainbow rings) and unwrapped velocity maps.
  2. Rotating the 3D PyDeck extrusion map showing deep red subsidence bowls around District 7 and Nhà Bè.
  3. Clicking on the Infrastructure Vulnerability table to highlight risk at Bến Thành Metro Terminal and Ba Son Bridge.
  4. Adjusting the forward-forecasting slider to year 2035.
* **Audio / Voiceover:**
  > *"On our interactive 3D console, urban planners can immediately visualize the hidden deformation field. Look at these deep red subsidence bowls in District 7 and Bình Tân, dropping at over 35 millimeters annually. Our AI automatically screens critical civil assets—alerting engineers that Metro Line 1 stations and major bridge abutments face severe differential settlement. With one click, planners can export standardized GeoJSON vectors directly into QGIS."*

#### [1:20 - 1:45] Scientific Validation & Community Impact
* **Visual:** Overlapping map layers showing soft Holocene clay thickness, industrial groundwater well locations, and InSAR subsidence hotspots matching perfectly.
* **Audio / Voiceover:**
  > *"Combining AI with geospatial earth science, we correlated our radar findings with geological drillings. The critical sinking hotspots align perfectly with 30-meter thick soft Holocene clay layers and heavy groundwater pumping. InSAR-SinkWatch provides the decisive empirical data cities need to stop illegal aquifer depletion and safeguard hundreds of thousands of residents."*

#### [1:45 - 2:00] Outro & The Team
* **Visual:** Team logo `visionspace_us`, GitHub repo URL, and the message: *"Empowering Coastal Resilience with NASA Open Radar Data."*
* **Audio / Voiceover:**
  > *"Built by team visionspace_us for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. Transforming space radar into ground-level resilience. Thank you!"*
